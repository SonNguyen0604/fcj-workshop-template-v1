Event 1 - Cloud Architect

Cloud Architect

Event information

  • Time: 09:00 - 12:00, 20/06/2026.
  • Location: Amazon Web Services Vietnam Office, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City.
  • Role: Member of a five-person competing team.

This was the program’s 7th event, named Cloud Architect. It took place shortly before the official internship period began on 22/06/2026 and served as an onboarding activity for the program.

Format and content

Two teams competed head-to-head and answered question sets with increasing difficulty. The team with the higher score advanced. If both teams were tied after the main questions, question 11 was used as a speed-based tie breaker.

The questions focused on Cloud and architecture design knowledge. Each team could use two tactical skills:

  • Minimum Risk - one use: for an uncertain answer; a wrong answer does not lose points, while a correct answer receives half of the question’s score.
  • Hope Star - one use: for the team’s most confident answer; a correct answer receives double points, while a wrong answer loses double points.

Personal experience

My team competed directly but did not reach the final round. The most useful part was having to discuss options very quickly before committing to an answer, especially when several architecture choices looked similar.

After my team stopped competing, I continued watching the remaining matches to observe how other teams analyzed requirements, divided technical responsibilities, and chose when to use the tactical skills.

Lessons learned

  • Cloud knowledge is more useful when connected to architecture context rather than memorized service names.
  • Under time pressure, assigning strengths across Networking, Compute, Database, and Security helps a team reason faster.
  • Architecture decisions should consider several factors instead of defaulting to a familiar service.
  • Wrong answers helped me identify areas to review before completing my High Availability project.

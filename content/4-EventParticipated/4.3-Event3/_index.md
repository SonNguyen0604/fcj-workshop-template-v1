### Main content

During this sharing session, teams presented the products/projects they had built. I was able to observe different approaches to architecture, demos, AWS service explanations, and result presentation.

Participants also shared their AWS certification study and exam experiences, including how they divided knowledge domains, used learning resources, practiced scenario-based questions, and managed exam time.

### What I learned

* A strong technical project needs a **clear architecture, a working demo, and explainable trade-offs**.
* Presentation quality strongly affects how easily an audience can understand a system.
* For AWS certification study, understanding service use cases is more important than memorizing definitions.
* Practical sharing gave me a clearer direction for continuing cloud learning after the internship.

### Connection to my project

After listening to other teams, I reviewed my workshop with a stronger focus on reproducibility: not only showing resources, but also documenting steps, testing/validation, and clean-up.
